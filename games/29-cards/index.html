♠ 29 CARDS ♥

YOUR TEAM 0 THEM 0 BEST BID 0

How to play
  • Teams: you and your Partner (opposite) against West and East. 32 cards (7 to Ace), 4 players, 8 tricks per hand.
  • Card order (high → low): J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7. Points: J = 3, 9 = 2, A = 1, 10 = 1. The last trick is worth 1 more, so each hand has 29 points in total.
  • Bidding: you see 4 cards first. Bid how many points your team will win (16–29) or pass. The highest bidder picks the trump suit, then the other 4 cards are dealt.
  • Play: follow the led suit if you can. If you can't, you may play any card; a trump card beats any other suit. Highest trump wins, otherwise the highest card of the led suit. The winner leads next.
  • Scoring: if the bidding team wins at least its bid it scores +1 game point, otherwise −1. First team to +5 wins the match; −5 loses it.
  • Simplified here: the trump suit is announced openly (the traditional game keeps it hidden until needed), and there are no pairs, doubles or redoubles.
